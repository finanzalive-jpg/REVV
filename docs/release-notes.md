**Your PC, revved up.**

This is an early preview of Revv: a lightweight Linux operating system with a classic desktop, made to bring older PCs back to life.

### How to try it
1. Download `revv-<version>-live-amd64.iso` below.
2. Write it to a USB stick (8 GB or more) with balenaEtcher or Rufus (*DD mode*).
3. Boot from the stick (F12, F11, F9 or Esc at power-on) and choose **Avvia Revv**.

Nothing is written to your disk. Requirements: 64-bit processor (2010 or newer), 4 GB RAM. If Secure Boot is on, turn it off for now.

### Please report
Tell us how it ran on your hardware: [open an issue](https://github.com/finanzalive-jpg/revv/issues/new) with the PC model, what worked and what did not.

Check the download with the `.sha256` file: `sha256sum -c revv-<version>-live-amd64.iso.sha256`.
